## IMPORTANT SEO PRINCIPLE

There is no legitimate way to guarantee the #1 Google position.
For a person-name search, the strongest signals usually come from:
- a clear custom domain
- consistent name/title across the site
- links from authoritative university pages
- LinkedIn and scholarly profiles linking back
- useful, substantive pages rather than keyword stuffing
- publications, talks, awards, and media pages that accumulate external links over time
