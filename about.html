SHORT BIO

Jaeyeon (Jae) Chung

Dr. Jae Chung is the William S. Mackey Jr. Distinguished Assistant Professor of Marketing at Rice University. Her research examines how consumers behave in digital and emerging markets, particularly when technology changes what people trust, value, choose, and believe.

She earned a Ph.D. and M.Phil. in Marketing and an M.S. in Marketing Science from Columbia Business School. She received her B.A. in Psychology, with Honors and Distinction, from the University of Michigan–Ann Arbor.
