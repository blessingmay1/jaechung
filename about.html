Short Bio

I am a marketing professor at Rice University. My research examines how consumers behave in digital and emerging markets, especially when technology changes what people trust, value, choose, and believe.

Research Perspective

Across my projects, I study recommendation systems, social platforms, influencers, digital ownership, virtual reality, AI, and market dynamics. I am particularly interested in connecting fundamental consumer psychology with consequential real-world market behavior.

This site is designed to clearly present you as a marketing scholar with recognizable digital-platform expertise while also making room for teaching, expert analysis, academic writing, consulting, and summer-program interests.
