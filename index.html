ABOUT

A behavioral scientist studying consumers in changing marketplaces.

MSI Young Scholar (2025).

I am a marketing professor at Rice University. My research focuses on how consumers navigate digital and emerging marketplace environments—especially when technology changes the signals people use to judge quality, credibility, value, and social influence.

Across my projects, I study recommendation systems, creators, social platforms, digital ownership, immersive experiences, AI, and market dynamics. I am particularly interested in research questions that connect fundamental consumer psychology with consequential real-world digital behavior.
