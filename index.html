Marketing · Rice University

I study how technology-mediated environments shape consumer identity, value, and choice.

My research combines experiments with natural shocks, marketplace data, and large-scale digital traces to understand what people trust, value, choose, and believe as markets evolve. My work appears in leading marketing and interdisciplinary journals, and related research has been featured in Harvard Business Review.

RESEARCH APPROACH & CAPABILITIES

Connecting real-world behavior with causal evidence.

I use complementary methods to study consequential questions about consumer identity, motivation, trust, and choice.

Experiments & Surveys

I design experiments, surveys, stimuli, and measures to isolate the mechanisms behind consumer decisions.

Field Research

I study behavior in real settings, including natural shocks and changing marketplace environments.

Marketplace & Text Analysis

I analyze transactional, social-media, marketplace, and open-ended textual data at scale.

Multi-Method Evidence

I bring field data and controlled studies together to test whether findings hold across settings.
